If something looks wrong

    June 2027, reviewed and opened — and June 2028 made from it

    This guide is for you, the coordinator: the person who sets up each session's subjects, teachers, prices and dates, the work the school's Google Forms used to do. June 2027 is already on the system, made from your 25 forms (17 O.L. and 8 A.S./A.L.): every subject, what each can be entered as, how it is studied, its teachers, and the exam fees from your latest fee lists. You do not build it. You check it, add what no form holds (the school's course fees and your dates), and open it to families. Next year you make June 2028 by copying it.

    21 stepsPart A: review the pre-loaded June 2027 and open it.
    13 stepsPart C: make June 2028 by copying June 2027.

    Part B, in between, shows what families and the desk see once the session is open; you do none of it. A few steps are the admin's, not yours: they are marked The admin. Taking payments is the desk's and the finance office's, never yours.

    How to read a step. Words shaded like this are written exactly as the screen writes them: look for those words. Where you are names the screen. The numbered lines are what to click or type, in order. You see says what the screen shows when it worked. Where your form had something to say, the yellow box quotes it. In the pictures, a red frame marks what to click; it is drawn on the picture only, never on your screen. Everything saves as you go: you can stop after any step and come back.

    About the pictures. They were taken on a practice copy of the system on its October 2026 version, set up exactly as this guide says. The teachers' names in them (T-ARABIC-OL-1, T-CHEMISTRY-OL-3-FORM…) stand in for the names on your forms; your screen shows the real names. The staff and the families are invented. The course fees (EGP 6,000 an O.L. subject, EGP 9,000 an A.S./A.L. subject), the exam fees typed by hand (EGP 9,500), the dates (pay by 15 January 2027, first lesson 1 November 2026) and the boards' entry deadlines are placeholders: type your own. The lists pasted are the school's Cambridge and Edexcel June 2026 lists, the newest it has; Part C pastes them again because June 2027's are not published yet.

    Before you start 3 steps, not counted

    What to have on your desk, the account the admin gives you, and the address of the system.

    0.1Have these at hand

    • The boards' fee lists, the PDFs the school receives: Cambridge's “Registration Fees” list and Edexcel's. The June 2027 lists are not published yet. The session already carries the June 2026 figures; pasting a list checks them against it and takes any figure that changed (step A9). When June 2027's arrive, you paste them the same way.
    • The school's course fee for each subject this cycle: the school's own figure, on top of the board's exam fee. No list holds it, so you type it.
    • Your dates: the day families should pay by, the first lesson (the refund weeks count from it) and, if you want one, a day reservations close.
    • Your forms and the links sheet, to compare with what the system shows. You will not need to copy anything from them.

    0.2The adminAn account for you

    Where you are: Team in the column on the left.

    1. Name: the coordinator's name.
    2. Email: the email she signs in with.
    3. Temp. password: a first password of at least 8 characters with a capital letter and a digit.
    4. Role: Coordinator. (The line under the form still describes the role's old duties. Since the change, the coordinator sets up the sessions, subjects, teachers, prices and dates, and reads who reserved and who paid.)
    5. Click Create Account, and give her the address below, her email and the password yourself.

    The desk gets its account the same way, with Role: Finance Officer.

    You see: Account created for and her email. She appears in the Staff list.

    0.3Open the system

    1. On a computer (the screens are wide; a laptop is fine), open Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
    2. Type this address and press Enter: https://subject-reservation-system.vercel.app

    After a quiet spell the first page can take up to a minute to appear. Wait for it; do not click again.

    There is no screen yet to change the temporary password: keep it to yourself.

    If something looks wrong

    Who to tell, by what went wrong. Nothing here needs you to work around the system.

    • The boards' lists: tell us (the system's team). A subject missing from Pick from the board's list…, a code that is wrong on a family's page, a line of a pasted list not recognised or left To check, a board rule that looks wrong (how long a result carries forward, how a one-paper retake is priced). We keep those lists; you never edit them.
    • A board's date: the admin, for a date still missing (Waiting for the admin); us, for a date already saved that turns out wrong.
    • An account or a password: the admin (Team).
    • Money: the finance office. A payment, a receipt, a refund, a family's balance. A price you change after families reserved asks first (Before this is saved): unpaid reservations take the new price and their families are told; paid ones are listed for the finance office to settle.
    • A screen that does not do what this guide says: us, with the step's number (A12, C4…) and what you saw.
