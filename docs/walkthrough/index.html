Rebuilding the demo

The June 2027 cycle, from a clean clone to the families

The school registered each cycle with a links sheet and one Google Form per subject — 25 for June 2027 — and took the money at the desk with no price on any form. This page builds that same cycle in the system and runs it: Part 1 installs the system from a clean clone; Part 2 is the admin, rebuilding the June 2027 cycle exactly as the forms describe it and running it — the desk, the money, the follow-up, the entries to the board; Part 3 is a student and Part 4 a parent, doing what the forms asked of them.

Every step names the label to click as the screen writes it (like this); beside it, in yellow, the school's own form says the same thing in its words; under it, the screen at that step. Every step was done in this order on a running system, and every screenshot was taken at its step (16 Oct 2026, the code of feature/walkthrough-cycle at the merge of main 9a38c3d). Names are placeholders; no school data, no phone number, no real person.

Part 1 — Set up from a clean clone

From nothing to a running system with its first admin. Every command below was run, in this order, on a fresh clone (16 Oct 2026, the code of feature/walkthrough-cycle after the merge of main 9a38c3d). The walkthrough's own run used the ports 3191 (API) and 3190 (web) and a database container on 5434 because 3000, 3001 and 5433 were taken on that machine; the commands here use the defaults.

The database

1.4Start PostgreSQL 17 in a container

Name the container and choose the port (here igcse-db on 5433; any free port works — the walkthrough used 5434):

docker run -d --name igcse-db -e POSTGRES_USER=audit -e POSTGRES_PASSWORD=auditpass \
  -e POSTGRES_DB=igcse_audit -p 127.0.0.1:5433:5432 postgres:17.5

This is the line of apps/api/test/README.md: the same container also serves the integration tests, which create their own database in it.

1.5Create the database

docker exec igcse-db createdb -U audit igcse_dev

A fresh database then holds the three boards (Pearson Edexcel: January, June, October, November; Cambridge International: June, November; OxfordAQA: January, June, November), the boards' 15 services, their entry rules, the 5 reminder rules and the 7 message texts — and no subject, session, setting or account.

Leave it running in its own terminal. The scheduler opens and closes sessions by their dates once a minute.

On another port: pnpm --filter web exec next dev --port 3190 (and set CORS_ORIGINS, APP_URL to match). Open http://localhost:3000.

The first admin

1.12Create an account

The admin is made from an ordinary account: sign up, then the seed promotes it. On the sign-in page Create one, then I'm a Parent.

1.13Sign up the first account

On Parent Registration:

  1. Full Name: School Admin
  2. Email Address: admin@igcse.local — the address the seed looks for
  3. Password and Confirm Password: at least 8 characters, an upper-case letter and a digit (the demo uses AdminPass1)
  4. Create Parent Account.

1.14The account is a parent's until the seed runs

The new account lands on a parent's Home ("Start by linking your child"). Do nothing here: the next step makes it the admin.

1.15Run the seed

pnpm --filter @repo/db seed
Seeding 17 subjects...
Seeding 2 registration sessions...
  [session] June 2026 (june) - active (created if missing)
  [session] November 2026 – January 2027 (winter) - draft (created if missing)
Looking for user admin@igcse.local to promote to admin...
  Promoted admin@igcse.local to admin.

It reads DATABASE_URL from apps/api/.env. Besides the promotion it makes 17 sample Cambridge IGCSE subjects (older syllabus codes such as 0610 Biology, not yet mapped on the Catalogue) and two sample sessions of 2026. Running it again changes nothing.

1.16Sign in as the admin

Sign Out, then on the sign-in page: Email address admin@igcse.local, Password, Sign in.

1.17The admin's Home

Home shows each session being prepared or open and what it still needs. Right after the seed it shows the seed's November 2026 – January 2027 session, a draft whose dates (July to October 2026) are already past.

1.18What the fresh database shows

Sessions: the seed's June 2026 session is closed by the scheduler's first tick; November 2026 – January 2027 stays a draft. Exam Catalogue: the three boards and the 17 sample subjects, "not mapped yet". Part 2 starts from here.

1.19The Catalogue after the seed

1.20Put the school's name on the sign-in page

Settings → The school → The school's name → Change: type the name in New value, a Reason, Save change. Only the admin may change it. The demo uses the placeholder Demo International School.

1.21The name is saved

1.22The sign-in page carries it

Every visitor now sees the school's name above the app's name on the sign-in, sign-up and password pages.

1.23Before going live

A local install is not a production one. Before the school uses it, work through SECURITY_AUDIT.md §6, the production checklist: NODE_ENV=production; a new random BETTER_AUTH_SECRET; HTTPS; CORS_ORIGINS and COOKIE_DOMAIN for the real addresses; CLIENT_IP_HEADER behind a proxy; the email provider (RESEND_API_KEY, EMAIL_FROM, required in production); R2 for uploads; TZ=Africa/Cairo for the API; one API process; REQUIRE_EMAIL_VERIFICATION never false; the payment providers stay off (InstaPay is checked by hand on the Finance Workbench). Replace every demo password, and do not run the demo script there.

Part 2 — The admin's journey: the June 2027 cycle, as the 25 forms describe it

The school registered June 2027 with a links sheet and 25 Google Forms — 17 O.L. and 8 A.S./A.L. (SCHOOL_FORMS.md §2.2, §2.3). Here the same cycle is built on the screens, in the order an admin works: the team, the catalogue, the settings, the session and its sheet, the fees, the dates, a look at the family's form, opening; then the running of the cycle. Teachers are placeholders (Teacher 1 … Teacher 33, numbered in the order the forms list them; "External Team" for the external teams). The school's course fees are in none of the artefacts (Q-14): the demo uses EGP 12,000 for an O.L. subject, 15,000 for a Cambridge A level, 4,000 per IAL unit and 4,000 for one paper. The boards' fees are the lists of SCHOOL_FORMS.md §3. Every demo account other than the admin has the password TestPass1.

What the screens could not do as the forms describe it

Found while building and driving this walkthrough; each is recorded in docs/features/WALKTHROUGH.md.

The seed. On a fresh database the seed's 17 sample subjects carry older Cambridge syllabus codes (0610 Biology …) and are not mapped (the catalogue's backfill ran before them), and its two sessions are of 2026: June 2026 closes at the scheduler's first tick; November 2026 – January 2027 stays a draft whose reserving ended on 1 Oct 2026. Home leads with that draft ("Being prepared … from 1 Jul 2026 to 1 Oct 2026", "Add the subjects") and no screen removes a draft session.
Adding a subject. The forms' subjects the database lacks are added only on Subjects (/admin/subjects), which the decisions of 16 Oct hid from the sidebar: the Catalogue maps subjects but cannot add one. Its form and its CSV still ask the old Registration Fee and Core columns that the session's fees and grade-10 core replaced.
Retiring samples. Deactivating a sample marked core is refused until "Remove core designation" is clicked; deactivated subjects still count in the Catalogue's "N subjects are not mapped yet" (16 after this walkthrough).
Arabic A.L. The second qualification under one subject (GCE 9AA0 beside IAL YAA01, "the teacher choice chooses the qualification") has no screen: only the item endpoint makes it (decision 4). The demo script adds it; the form then shows and edits both.
One paper only. A one-paper retake is priced with the syllabus's whole board fee (Q-13): Cambridge Paper 41/42 with 10,850; Edexcel Mathematics 1H alone with 9,200 where the list prices a paper at 4,600. The fee list must name the syllabus twice ("0970 CX" for All papers, "0970" for the paper).
Form options with no shape of their own. Computer Science O.L.'s "Paper 41/42" (left from last year's form: 0984 has no Paper 4) is not offered; Physics A.S./A.L.'s three A.2. choices are one route with the sitting named by the family; the forms' separate "Self Study (ONLY 2nd entry)" Yes/No question is the entry choice "Retake, self-study".
Fee lists and codes. The lists in hand are June 2026's (June 2027's were not published); the rows no list has are typed as placeholders, not published. Oxford's and Global Citizenship's codes are the school's, not the boards'; Environmental Management, Global Perspectives and Global Citizenship have no option code, so their entries would be flagged.
Home after an InstaPay reference. The family's Home keeps counting the lines whose transfer awaits verification (EGP 60,900) and its Pay now opens the checkout of all three, which stops at "Payment already started": the other board's line is paid from My Registrations (tick, Pay Selected).
The desk and a transfer in progress. The desk's "To collect now" lists and ticks lines whose InstaPay transfer is waiting for finance, and "Owes now" counts them; Collect is then refused ("These subjects already have a transfer in progress — confirm it or reject it in the Finance Workbench first").
Codes on families' screens. The statement's payments read "(cash)", "(instapay)"; notifications "Payment of EGP 21200.00 via in_school" and "Escrow balance credited … New balance: EGP 16850.00"; Pending Requests "16850.00 EGP"; the workbench's dialog "39700.00 EGP". In Arabic the statement's receipt states ("With Parent", "Returned to School") stay English.
The school's name. The reservation slip and the receipt print "IGCSE Subject Reservation System", not the school's name (decision 15 covers the sign-in pages).
Words on staff screens. Settings' refund policy card names an internal file, "(SCHOOL_FORMS.md §2.1)"; the exception's sentence shows "by 2026-12-20" and "1 due dates moved."; My Registrations' Request Drop and Drop dialogs have no dialog role.
The seed's draft elsewhere. The seed's stale November 2026 – January 2027 draft also shows on a student's record as an "exam series open now" ("Being prepared · May register").
Profile. The admin's Profile offers "Linked Parents — View and manage parent connections", a student's link.
Documents. README.md says uploads fail without R2 keys, but in development they go to LOCAL_UPLOAD_DIR; .env.example still lists AUTO_GRADE_PROGRESSION (removed by F0a) and lacks LOCAL_UPLOAD_DIR.
